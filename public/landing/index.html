What is Zephric?＋

Zephric is an early-stage company building an AI-native financial operations platform for construction. The intended workflow brings billing documents, reconciliation, and human review together. The screens on this site are interactive product concepts, not a live commercial platform.

Who is Zephric for?＋

General contractors, subcontractors, construction finance teams, controllers, and CFOs who manage payment applications, invoices, and supporting financial documents.

Does it replace our accounting software?＋

No. Zephric is intended to work alongside your existing construction management and accounting software, helping with the review and reconciliation work around those systems.

Does Zephric work only with Procore?＋

No. The vision is broader than any one platform. A Procore integration is planned, and other connection options are being explored. No live integration or official partnership is claimed.

Which documents are planned?＋

Payment applications, subcontractor invoices, Schedules of Values, contracts, change orders, retainage records, and supporting documents such as lien waivers where applicable. Availability and formats will be shaped with design partners.

How does human review work?＋

The planned workspace places extracted financial data alongside original documents and reconciliation findings. Reviewers can inspect evidence, correct information, and resolve exceptions. Your team retains responsibility for approval decisions.

Can teams outside the United States participate?＋

Yes. We’re initially focusing on the United States and India and welcome conversations globally. Local billing practices, tax requirements, retention terms, and supporting documents will need to be evaluated for each market.

How do we become a design partner?＋

Use the form above to tell us about your team and current workflow. The founders can then discuss your challenges and whether the early product direction is a fit. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee product access.
